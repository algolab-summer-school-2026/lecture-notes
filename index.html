About

The AlgoLab Summer School on Fault-Tolerant Quantum Computing brings together students, postdocs, and researchers for an intensive program of lectures on the latest developments in fault-tolerant quantum computing. For more information about the Summer School, please visit the official website.

This page archives all publicly available lecture materials. Materials marked as unavailable were not cleared for public release by the speaker.

For questions about the school, contact ecole.iq@usherbrooke.ca.
